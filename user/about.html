About Us

Bite Co. Official is a small coffee cart business serving handcrafted coffee, specialty lattes, and refreshing beverages made with quality ingredients. We are passionate about delivering delicious, affordable drinks with friendly service, making every cup a memorable experience. Whether it's your daily coffee fix or a special celebration, we also offer coffee cart party packages for birthdays, weddings, corporate events, and other occasions to make your event even more special.
